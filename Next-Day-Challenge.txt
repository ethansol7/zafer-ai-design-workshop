ZAFER / THE NEXT-DAY CHALLENGE

Choose one simple, noncritical desk project or variation.
Allow 30–45 minutes. Work from a new brief, not a copied picture.

1. Name the user, problem, and one success criterion.
2. Measure one real interface and record units.
3. Ask AI to identify missing facts; do not accept invented dimensions.
4. Sketch a direction and create or revise your native CAD model.
5. Change a driving dimension and explain why.
6. Measure the result, save the native source, and export a mesh.
7. Reopen the export and check its overall scale.
8. Write one physical test and one next revision.

Share: a before/after image, the native source, export, and a short README.

Success is a reasoned, editable starting point. It is not a claim that any object can be engineered or validated in an hour.
