What does this check tell me?

It compares your phone’s nominal thickness at the chosen lean with the pocket width, using a 1 mm teaching allowance. It does not prove stability, strength, rounded-corner clearance, case-button clearance or printability. Phone width is shown for visual comparison; side overhang is allowed. Measure, slice, print a small test, and revise.
